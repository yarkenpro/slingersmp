💻 Commands

Handy commands to get around the world fast.

/home

Teleport straight back to your set home base.

/sethome

Set your current spot as your home before you go exploring.

/tpa <player>

Request to teleport to another player. They have to accept.

/tphere <player>

Ask another player to teleport to you. Great for teaming up.

/tpaccept

Accept an incoming teleport request from someone.

/spawn

Head back to the main spawn point of the server.

…and more! Type /help in-game to see the full list.
